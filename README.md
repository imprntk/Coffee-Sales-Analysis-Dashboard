#📊 Project Overview #
This repository contains an interactive Coffee Sales Dashboard built to analyze and visualize sales performance across different coffee varieties, customer segments, and geographical locations. The project utilizes historical data (2019–2022) to provide actionable insights into consumer behavior and sales trends.

#🚀 Key Features #
Total Sales Over Time: A multi-line chart comparing sales performance across four major bean types: Arabica, Excelsa, Liberica, and Robusta.

Geographical Insights: A bar chart visualizing total sales generated in the United States, Ireland, and the United Kingdom.

Customer Analysis: Identification of the Top 5 Customers by total spend to help target high-value clients.

Interactive Slicers: Dynamic filtering capabilities based on:

Roast Type: Light, Medium, and Dark.

Size: 0.2kg, 0.5kg, 1.0kg, and 2.5kg.

Loyalty Status: Segmenting data based on whether customers hold a loyalty card.

Temporal Navigation: A custom timeline slicer to drill down into specific years and months between 2019 and 2022.

#🛠️ Tools Used #
Microsoft Excel: Primary tool for data processing and visualization.

Power Query: (If applicable) Used for cleaning and transforming the raw sales data.

Pivot Tables & Charts: Used to aggregate data and create the visual components of the dashboard.

Slicers & Timelines: Implemented for an interactive user experience.

#📈 Key Insights from the Dashboard #
Sales Trends: Sales fluctuate significantly by month, allowing for the identification of seasonal peaks.

Market Dominance: The United States represents the largest market share compared to Ireland and the UK.

Product Performance: Different bean types show varying popularity, with specific spikes in Arabica and Robusta during peak periods.

Open the file in Microsoft Excel (2016 or newer recommended for full slicer functionality).

Use the Timeline at the top to select specific date ranges.

Apply filters using the Slicers on the right (Roast Type, Size, Loyalty Card) to see how specific variables impact total sales.
